Getting Started: A 4-Step Walkthrough

Follow these steps to operate the tool in your workspace:

  1. Navigate to the Protect PDF interface on our website.
  2. Choose your target file and drag it directly into the drop zone, or use the file explorer selector.
  3. Configure the target properties or output values specifically suited to your project.
  4. Click the active process trigger and download your newly compiled file to your downloads directory.
